## Interpretation and decision

UART/controller execution and basic activity checks passed. Functional simulation, legal placement, positive ordinary timing and activity flags did not establish successful PUF replication. The pilot retains the original RTL and relative geometry, but independently routed instances are not identical copies of the preserved physical implementation. Even core 0, which retained its LUT/register coordinates, changed physical context and routing; the earlier 28.x% behavior cannot be assigned to it. The cause of the constant responses has not been established.

Do not expand this implementation to 128 cores, hide constant cores, whiten outputs, or repeat unchanged broad collections. Preserve it as diagnostic evidence and retain the original single-core bitstream as the empirical reference.

The next engineering investigation should verify implemented challenge delivery to every core and compare the launch, initialization/clear, edge-lock/arbiter capture and terminal/feedback loading/routes of a responsive core (1) with a constant core (0 or 4). Internal route matching alone cannot explain these results: core 0 has small modeled internal mismatch but constant responses. A bounded control with one core active at a time can test whether concurrent excitation contributes; it must be labeled a different physical/operating experiment and cannot be assumed to fix the issue. Choose a concrete diagnostic hypothesis before another build; validate that control before committing to an array-wide redesign.

Machine-readable per-core statistics and raw word histogram are saved beside the CSV as `.analysis.json`. The original preservation checkpoint remains unchanged. This result supersedes the smoke test's provisional permission to characterize; it does not invalidate the smoke test's limited protocol/activity findings.
