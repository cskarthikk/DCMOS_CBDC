Aggregate: 16896 ones /40960=41.25%. There are 1854 constant and 194 mixed core/pattern combinations out of 2048, with five majority ties. Sum of smaller response counts is 740; this is a descriptive within-run statistic, not BER against independently enrolled truth. A mixed pattern means at least one response changed during its 20 readings. A constant pattern is not certified stable under other conditions.

All eight cores produce both values across the sampled vectors. The constant-core failure of the legacy pilot is absent in this broader set. Core 5 is especially promising on the two observed criteria of balance and within-run consistency (48.96% ones, 12 mixed patterns); core 0 also approaches 50%. Bias remains visible in cores 6 and 7, and cores 1/2 have the most mixed patterns. Do not hide these cores or label the aggregate unbiased.

187 distinct raw words occurred, including within-pattern variation. This is not an independent-word entropy estimate. The aggregate ones ratio across eight independently configured cores is not the paper's 128-bit word-balance distribution or a directly equivalent statistic to the old one-core mixed-parity 28.x% collection. Architectural changes, accepted parity domain, challenge sets and response width differ.

The first 32 vectors exactly match the earlier Parallel/8 diagnostic. Comparing their per-core majority responses across separate collections gives five changes among 254 comparable core/pattern combinations; two ties are excluded. Changed pairs (core, zero-based pattern): (2,1), (4,4), (4,19), (6,22), (5,30). This is useful limited same-setting repeat evidence, not an independent confirmation of all 256 vectors or across power/temperature. The aggregate increase from 38.55% in the diagnostic to 41.25% here comes from a larger pool with additional vectors; it must not be called a temporal improvement.

## Decision

Keep this exact bitstream and Parallel/8 setting as the current eight-core development candidate. The data justify moving from routing experimentation into enrollment and independent validation work. Do not retune to chase 50%, claim final reliability, or scale to 128 yet. Retain all raw observations and report per-core bias/mixed patterns/ties. Enroll reference responses and any stability-based exclusion policy using designated enrollment data, then validate the complete frozen policy on a separate confirmation collection; do not use that validation to silently refilter or balance outputs. Document selection yield and excluded patterns if enrollment is introduced. BCH/hash integration belongs after the raw response behavior and reconstruction requirements are established.

Machine-readable statistics: the capture's `.analysis.json` sibling. Original checkpoint and all previous experimental outputs remain preserved.
