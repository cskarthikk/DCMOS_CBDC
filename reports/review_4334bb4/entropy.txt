------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   SCIENTIFIC EVALUATION & CRYPTOGRAPHIC LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. INDEPENDENCE CAVEAT: Summing marginal core entropies assumes spatial independence.
   Because real FPGA cores share power distribution networks and die substrate,
   true joint min-entropy H_inf(C0..C30) may be less than the marginal sum.
2. LIVE DEMO REALITY: The live demo (`crypto_demo.ps1`) uses 8 challenges,
   accumulating 16 nominal 16-bit random secrets (256 nominal key bits).
   This serves as an operational proof-of-concept for the Dodis code-offset
   protocol, but does NOT mathematically prove 256 bits of independent physical
   silicon entropy.
3. CONCLUSION: The code-offset scheme eliminates the direct 16-candidate exploit
   present in the legacy parity scheme. However, rigorous quantification of retained
   physical entropy and inter-challenge correlation remains an open research topic.
