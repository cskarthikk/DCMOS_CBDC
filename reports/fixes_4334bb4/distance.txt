Total Physical Cores Evaluated : 64 parallel cores
Total Core Pairs Evaluated     : 2,016 combinations (C(64, 2))
Total Pairwise Bit Comparisons : 516,096 bit evaluations
Pairs with Matching Challenges : 17 / 516096 (0.0033%)
Mean Cross-Core Hamming Dist.  : 50.23% (Range: [14.45% .. 85.16%])
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   CRITICAL METHODOLOGICAL DISTINCTION (UNIQUENESS VS CROSS-CORE DISTANCE)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. NOT SAME-CHALLENGE UNIQUENESS: In `validation_1.csv`, each core received an
   independently randomized 16-bit challenge per pattern. Therefore, only 17
   comparisons shared identical inputs. The 50.23% HD reflects cross-core
   and inter-challenge decorrelation, NOT same-challenge physical uniqueness.
2. TRUE INTER-DEVICE UNIQUENESS: Requires measuring multiple physical FPGA boards
   under the exact same challenges. On a single development board, true inter-chip
   uniqueness cannot be directly evaluated.
3. INTER-CHALLENGE DISTANCE: On this single chip, evaluating distinct 64-bit response
   words across different challenges yields a mean HD of 41.16% (range 21.9%..60.9%).
